## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: the behaviour is specific (distance from campus, semester pin, "or best offer", the share flow that hands a story image to Instagram/Snapchat); the visual language is a stock rentals template recoloured green and gold. Nothing on screen draws "The Campus Notice Board", and the friendly/student-made/lively mood lives only in a few lines of copy. UVM colours + UVM sign-in + the name, with no "not affiliated" line anywhere in the app, push it toward looking official, which PRODUCT.md rules out.

Deterministic scan: 7 findings over app/index.php, app/map.php, app/post.php and the three includes. All 3 warnings are false positives (two `<img id="modalImage" src="">` placeholders that are hidden until openModal() sets src; one "<img>" inside a PHP comment in map.php:20). 4 advisories: off-ramp 0.8rem in inline styles at post.php:372, 452, 522, 572 (0.8rem also appears 14× in style.css, so it is a missing step, not stray drift). The detector's verification also caught a bug the review missed: #modalImage gets two error listeners (app.js:1950 and 1966), so one transient failure shows "Image not available" when a retry would have worked. No overlays: no browser.

## Overall Impression

A careful, well-guarded app wearing a generic coat. The single biggest opportunity is the phone: Browse opens on a form instead of the board, and the listing view (where the decision is made) is a desktop dialog shrunk down.

## What's Working

- The share flow fits how students pass listings around: the story image goes to the OS share sheet as a file, unusable tiles hide, desktop falls back to download, and the ?id= deep link drops stale filters so the shared card always opens.
- Every card carries the same comparable facts in the same places, in compact domain wording ("2 bd · 1.5 ba · 1 roommate", "~$150/mo utils"); the two empty states are distinct and well written.
- Guardrails and a11y basics: geocoder-only addresses, deactivated semester kept selectable, roommate fields hidden at 0, hover gated on (hover:hover), reduced motion honoured, :focus-visible rings, cards answer Enter/Space, utilities use colour and text.

## Priority Issues

[P1] Phone Browse opens on a form, not the board. The filter card (two sliders, a select, 7 chips) fills roughly the first screen; every change is a full reload and a second chip tap during it is dropped; the count claims active filters after any touch; the price slider runs $0-$1,350 while listings run $800-$1,350. Fix: at ≤768px a sticky one-line summary bar opening a bottom sheet with Apply + live count; slider bounds from the data; submit price/distance only when moved; List/Map toggle that carries the query. Command: /impeccable layout

[P1] The listing view fails on the phone. Not full-screen; portrait photos letterboxed (object-fit: contain in a 5:3 box); no swipe, unlabeled 8px dots; close scrolls away and Back exits; Email/Call only at the top; distance from campus missing; address is plain text. Fix: full-screen sheet ≤600px with sticky top (close, share) and bottom (price/mo, Email, Call) bars; scroll-snap swipe gallery with a counter; history state so Back closes; a facts row (price/mo · walk · semester · size); address as a maps link. Command: /impeccable adapt

[P1] Posting can dead-end and loses work. Autocomplete shows no "searching"/no-match state and has no ARIA, yet submit is blocked until a suggestion is picked; no drop-a-pin fallback; map preview below Submit on phones and pre-pinned on campus; no busy state on submit; an error on a new post re-renders an empty form; re-picking photos replaces the batch; remove-photo X is hover-only and deletes immediately. Fix: searching/no-match states with a draggable pin; map under the address field; "Uploading 5 photos…"; repopulate from $_POST; accumulating photos with visible remove; ARIA combobox. Command: /impeccable harden

[P1] The card emphasises what differs least. The gold semester badge is the loudest thing and reads SPRING 2027 on 33/34 cards; price is a 0.85rem badge without "/mo"; distance is text-muted 0.8rem (3.43:1); 5-7 near-universal tags at 0.7rem; the purple "Looking for: …" tag reads as a requirement though the form calls it a preference; "or best offer" at 0.6rem. Fix: one fact line ≥1rem ("$1,150/mo · 6 min walk · 3 bd · 2 roommates"); semester badge only when the set spans semesters; 2-3 rarest tags + "+N"; a neutral "Prefers…" tag; no card text below 0.75rem or in text-muted. Command: /impeccable distill

[P2] Trust is never shown at the two high-stakes moments. Contacting: a muted "Posted by jdoe12", no posted date, a draft that greets a NetID. Posting: no "only signed-in UVM students see your address" note; no "not affiliated with UVM" line. Fix: poster line near the price ("Posted by Maya · UVM student · 3 days ago"); address-privacy note under the field; warmer draft with an editable signature; mailto fallback; footer disclaimer. Command: /impeccable clarify

## Minor Observations

- Modal close uses rgba(0,0,0,.5), breaking the Slate Ink rule; .btn hover isn't gated on (hover:hover).
- Dismissed announcement returns on every filter reload.
- Share-sheet note says "your contact details" even when sharing someone else's listing.
- No-match empty state blames price/distance when A/C or Pets chips (6/34 each) are the usual cause.
- Map pins overlap: 15 listings in 7 clusters within ~100m, no clustering.
- Stated layout-transition: the gallery collapses via padding-top (style.css:747).
